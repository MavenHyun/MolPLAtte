# --- chemistry -------------------------------------------------------------
# Upper bound is load-bearing.  Five of the eleven feature tables in
# molpallete_prep/mol_features.py enumerate an RDKit enum (ChiralType,
# HybridizationType, BondType, BondDir, BondStereo) and store the *index* into
# that enumeration.  A release that adds or reorders an enum member shifts every
# index and silently invalidates the embedding indices of an already-built
# corpus.  See the comment in pyproject.toml before bumping.
rdkit>=2024.3.1,<2027
